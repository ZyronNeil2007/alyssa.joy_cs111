Magnifico

2003 · Filipino family drama

Synopsis

In a small town in Laguna, Magnifico is a kind and clever boy from a family with very little. Whenever someone around him is in trouble, from his grandmother to his brother to his little sister, he finds a way to help, and he never asks for anything back.
