Joke Time

Five jokes for when the store is quiet. Tap a receipt to see the punchline.

ResiboNo. 1

Why did the sari-sari store owner bring a ladder to work?

Because the prices were too high!

ResiboNo. 2

Bakit laging masaya ang tindahan? (Why is the store always so happy?)

Kasi lahat ng problema, tingi-tingi lang! (Every problem comes in small sachets.)

ResiboNo. 3

What do you call a sleeping bull at the store?

A bulldozer!

ResiboNo. 4

Why did the cookie go to the doctor?

It was feeling crumby.

ResiboNo. 5

Why was the math book sad?

It had too many problems.
