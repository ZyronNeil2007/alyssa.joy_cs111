# 😂 Jokes Collection

A collection of random, relatable, and painfully accurate jokes about student life, responsibilities, and everyday struggles.

---

## 1. Brain.exe Has Stopped Working

**Q:** Why am I always tired in class?

**A:** Because my brain is still loading... ⏳

## 2. Financial Crisis

**Q:** Why is my wallet always empty?

**A:** Because every time I open it, there's nothing inside. 💸

## 3. The Night Owl

**Me:** I'll sleep early tonight.

**Also me at 2 AM:** "One more video!" 📱

## 4. Online 24/7

**Friend:** Why are you always online?

**Me:** Because my responsibilities are offline. 💀

## 5. Future Plans

**Friend:** What's your plan for the future?

**Me:** Survive today first. We'll talk about tomorrow later. 😭

## 6. The Power of Smiling

**Friend:** Why are you always smiling?

**Me:** Because crying doesn't solve my problems, and neither does smiling, so why not? 😂
