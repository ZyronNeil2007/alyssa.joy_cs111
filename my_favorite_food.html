Lasang Sarap

My Favorite Food

The dishes I never get tired of, and how to cook my number one.

Photos

Adobong Manok

Serves 4 · About 1 hour

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into serving pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole peppercorns
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Cooking instructions

  1. Mix the chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves and peppercorns. Let it sit for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and brown the chicken on all sides.
  3. Pour in the marinade and the water. Bring to a boil without stirring.
  4. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 35 to 40 minutes until tender.
  5. Uncover and let the sauce reduce until it just coats the chicken.
  6. Serve hot with plenty of steamed rice.
