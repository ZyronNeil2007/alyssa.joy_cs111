Adobong Manok

Serves 4 · About 1 hour

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into serving pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole peppercorns
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Cooking instructions

  1. Mix the chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves and peppercorns. Let it sit for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and brown the chicken on all sides.
  3. Pour in the marinade and the water. Bring to a boil without stirring.
  4. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 35 to 40 minutes until tender.
  5. Uncover and let the sauce reduce until it just coats the chicken.
  6. Serve hot with plenty of steamed rice.

Lumpiang Togue

Serves 4 · About 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 pack bean sprouts (togue)
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp oyster sauce
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Spring roll wrappers
  • Cooking oil for frying

Cooking instructions

  1. Sauté garlic and onion in hot oil until fragrant.
  2. Add the vegetables and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until just tender.
  3. Season with soy sauce, oyster sauce and black pepper.
  4. Let the filling cool slightly before wrapping into spring roll wrappers.
  5. Deep-fry until golden brown and crispy.
  6. Serve with sweet chili sauce or vinegar dip.

Spaghetti

Serves 4 · About 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 250 g ground beef or pork
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated cheese for topping

Cooking instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water until al dente, then drain.
  2. In a pan, sauté garlic and onion until soft.
  3. Add the ground meat and cook until browned.
  4. Stir in tomato sauce, tomato paste, bell pepper, sugar, salt and pepper.
  5. Simmer for 10 to 15 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  6. Mix with the spaghetti and top with grated cheese before serving.

Silit Intestine

Serves 3 · About 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 kg pork intestines, cleaned
  • 3 tbsp vinegar
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp calamansi juice
  • 1 chili, sliced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Cooking oil

Cooking instructions

  1. Boil the cleaned intestines until tender, then drain.
  2. Heat oil and sauté garlic and onion until aromatic.
  3. Add the intestines and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Pour in the vinegar and let it simmer briefly.
  5. Season with calamansi, chili, salt and pepper.
  6. Serve hot with rice and a dipping sauce if desired.

Mustard Greens with Egg

Serves 2 · About 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch mustard greens, chopped
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1/2 cup water
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Cooking instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add the mustard greens and cook for 1 to 2 minutes.
  3. Pour in a little water and let it steam until tender.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Push the vegetables to one side and crack in the eggs.
  6. Scramble the eggs gently and mix together before serving.
